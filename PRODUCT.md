# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are Marathi-speaking police, army, SRPF and competitive-exam aspirants and their parents. Academy administrators manage admissions, media, attendance, tests, fees and notices.

## Product Purpose

Shivrakshak Career Academy presents the academy professionally, converts visitors into admission enquiries, and operates the day-to-day student workflow from one admin area.

## Operating Context

The public site is used primarily on mobile and laptop browsers. Admin work includes replacing landing-page photographs, uploading videos, publishing notices, recording face-assisted/manual/online attendance, and managing online tests.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js, React, TypeScript and Supabase.
- Marathi-first public content with clear mobile behavior.
- All public photography and video slots must be replaceable from the admin panel.
- Social/contact channels include YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, phone and email.
- Face attendance must ask for camera permission and provide a safe manual fallback.
- No invented selection statistics or testimonials should be presented as verified facts.

## Brand Commitments

The academy name and shield mark stay recognizable. The user-provided recruitment poster is the binding composition reference: warm cream background, saffron actions, deep navy structure, bold Marathi headline and authentic training photography.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the academy logo, admission/admin workflows and academy contact details. Placeholder photography may be used until the administrator uploads real academy photographs.

## Product Principles

- Trust before decoration.
- Mobile and laptop receive equal care.
- Admin-editable content instead of hard-coded media.
- Clear Marathi actions and recoverable errors.
- Student data remains private by default.
