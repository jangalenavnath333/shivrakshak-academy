# Design System

## Direction

An editorial recruitment prospectus translated to the web: cream paper, disciplined navy bands, saffron calls to action, olive supporting details and sunrise training photography.

## Typography

Use the Devanagari-capable sans stack from the application. Headlines are heavy and compact; body copy remains relaxed and readable. No gradient text and no decorative eyebrow labels.

## Color

- Ink: `#071b2d`
- Saffron: `#ed6508`
- Olive: `#40591b`
- Paper: `#fffaf1`
- Muted paper: `#f4efe5`

## Composition

The first viewport is a split editorial hero with copy on the left and real training imagery on the right. Proof features overlap the hero edge. Course cards use landscape photographs. Dense operational screens remain simple, light and scan-friendly.

## Responsive behavior

At mobile widths the navigation becomes a drawer, hero copy precedes the photograph, proof items scroll or wrap, course cards become a single column, and admin navigation becomes horizontally accessible rather than squeezing the content.

## Media demos

Feature demonstrations are poster-first: show an intentional 16:9 preview with one clear play action before loading embedded or hosted video. Keep loading, failure, retry and return-to-poster states inside the media frame so the card remains stable and every failure is recoverable.

Synthetic demonstrations must be identified next to the media in plain language and must not imply that depicted people are real students. On narrow screens, demonstration cards collapse to one column; controls and labels move into the content flow rather than overlapping or competing with fixed actions.
